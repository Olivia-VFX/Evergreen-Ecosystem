Where your support goes

Funds are used in this order, building from the ground up.

  1. 1

    Parent/Teacher Portal

    First, we develop our existing infrastructure: the Portal backend, including the ZPD Diagnostics Dashboard that highlights "Frustration Zones" for educators, and teacher-designed activities that help move students into the ZPD.

  2. 2

    The ethical AI guide

    Next, development and API costs, so the guide's support is optimal and consistent across the entire Ecosystem.

  3. 3

    Play-testing and research

    We invest in play-testing to check our games create the outcomes we designed, such as proving that discovery-led play improves long-term understanding and knowledge transfer.

  4. 4

    New subjects

    Finally, we scale the Ecosystem to new subjects while keeping the same core principles.

Common questions

What is Evergreen+?

Evergreen+ is our premium tier, with features like detailed insights and offline access. It's also home to our unique ethical, research-backed AI guide experience, which manages the Zone of Proximal Development with open-ended questions.

Is the core Ecosystem free?

Yes. The base Ecosystem, including access to our AI guide, is available to everyone so that cost never blocks effective learning.

Who receives sponsored licences?

Sponsored Evergreen+ licences go to underprivileged communities and schools.

Will the AI replace teachers?

No. Teachers and carers remain at the heart of education. Our tools support them with insights and activities, and the AI guide asks questions rather than giving easy answers.

Is this research-based?

Every design choice is grounded in cognitive and educational research, including the Cognitive Theory of Multimedia Learning (CTML) and the Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD).
