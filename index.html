A research-backed learning loop

Every game follows the same six steps, grounded in the Cognitive Theory of Multimedia Learning and the Zone of Proximal Development.

DiscoverExplore and ask
UnderstandMake sense of it
RecordStore your knowledge
RetrieveRecall
ApplyUse it for real
ReflectWork out where you succeeded or failed

Our real-world activities set us apart from apps that chase screen-time hours: the goal is learning that carries beyond the screen.

Aligned with UN SDG 4

We make our research-backed AI guide available to everyone, removing financial barriers to effective learning. A donor model then subsidises the advanced tools for those who need them most, promoting lifelong learning and universal education.

Where your support goes

  1. Parent/Teacher Portal. The ZPD Diagnostics Dashboard highlights "Frustration Zones", with teacher-designed activities to help students into their ZPD, and parents can help improve their childs learning through real-world activities.
  2. The ethical AI guide. Development and API costs, so support is consistent across the whole Ecosystem.
  3. Play-testing and research. Proving that discovery-led play improves long-term understanding and transfer.
  4. New subjects. Scaling the Ecosystem with the same core principles.
