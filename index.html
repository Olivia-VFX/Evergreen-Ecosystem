Melodic Meadows: Triolet Tulips

Wander onto a floating island where an old stone ruin has been reclaimed by plants. Your guide, a friendly deer, keeps the flower shop inside. Press a note, grow a tulip, and find the chords hiding in the meadow, with no streaks and no pressure.

  • Discover first. Explore colours and sounds, then learn what they mean.
  • The Flower Shop. Retrieval lives in its own space with a no-failure mechanic, so there's no fear of getting it wrong.
  • Parent/Teacher Portal. Data-driven insights plus real-world activities that move learning off the screen.

Aligned with UN SDG 4

We make our research-backed AI guide available to everyone, removing financial barriers to effective learning. A donor model then subsidises the advanced tools for those who need them most, promoting lifelong learning and universal education.

Where your support goes

  1. Parent/Teacher Portal. The ZPD Diagnostics Dashboard highlights "Frustration Zones", with teacher-designed activities to help students into their ZPD.
  2. The ethical AI guide. Development and API costs, so support is consistent across the whole Ecosystem.
  3. Play-testing and research. Proving that discovery-led play improves long-term understanding and transfer.
  4. New subjects. Scaling the Ecosystem with the same core principles.
